About Indie Stortford

Indie Stortford is a small, independent proof of concept to help residents and visitors discover locally owned and regional independent businesses in Bishop's Stortford.

How businesses are selected

Businesses are researched individually and may be submitted by their owners or suggested by residents. We distinguish confirmed information from details still requiring checks. Inclusion is not an endorsement or a ranking. Please check opening hours, availability and other details with businesses directly.

Corrections and submissions

To suggest a business, correct an entry or ask about a listing, email danielthenews@gmail.com. Listings are manually reviewed; requests do not guarantee inclusion.

How we plan to fund the project

The directory is free to use. We may introduce clearly identified advertising in future to help cover costs. Adverts are not currently part of the directory. Any future advertising will be subject to applicable privacy and consent requirements.
