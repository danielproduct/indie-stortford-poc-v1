Privacy information

Last reviewed: 16 November 2026. Draft for pre-launch verification.

Indie Stortford is a local independent-business directory proof of concept. For privacy questions, contact danielthenews@gmail.com.

Business enquiries

Clicking our business-enquiry link opens your own email application. If you send an email, your email provider transmits the message, and we receive the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to you and review the relevant listing. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Visitor feedback

The feedback survey is provided by Google Forms. The desktop version embeds that form and the mobile version links to it. Google may process information when the form loads or you submit it, in accordance with its own terms and privacy information.

Hosting and technical data

Our hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and device/browser information, for website delivery, operations and security. The public hosting provider may change as we prepare the commercial launch.

Advertising and analytics

No Google AdSense advertising or advertising cookies have been activated by this launch-preparation work. Before enabling advertising or additional analytics, this page and the relevant consent controls must be updated to reflect what is actually deployed.

Your choices

You can use the directory without creating an account. You do not have to submit the feedback form or send an email. To enquire about personal information supplied in an email or request a correction, contact the address above.
